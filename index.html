Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What does Bookmark of the Day do?

It opens a random link from your browser bookmarks so saved pages are easier to rediscover. You can filter picks by website, shuffle, or set a reminder.

Will I see the same bookmark over and over?

The Chrome extension tracks its picks and avoids repeats until it has gone through the eligible bookmarks in your current rotation.

Does the Chrome extension upload my bookmarks?

No. The Chrome extension reads bookmarks through Chrome’s bookmarks API and stores its rotation locally in your browser. It does not need an account.
